Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

On average, residential installations reduce annual electricity expenses by 60% to 80%. For commercial entities with large daytime energy consumption, our custom PV arrays frequently pay for themselves entirely within 3.5 to 5 years, delivering free clean energy thereafter.

Photovoltaic cells operate on ambient solar radiation, generating power even in overcast skies. Combined with an Energix Lithium-Iron-Phosphate battery bank, surplus power gathered during peak daylight hours is stored and automatically powers your facility throughout the night.

Standard residential installations are completed within 1 to 2 days without disrupting your home's power supply. Commercial installations range from 1 to 3 weeks depending on the roof area and utility transformer connections.

Every Energix installation includes a 25-Year Linear Power Output Warranty on solar panels, a 10-Year Comprehensive Warranty on Hybrid Inverters and Battery Packs, and a 10-Year Workmanship Warranty on all roof penetrations and waterproofing seals.
